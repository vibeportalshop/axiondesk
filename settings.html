Your private trade logs, journal entries, and AI analysis sessions are stored under your unique user ID. They are not visible to any other user.

We do not sell, share, or monetise your personal data. We do not store broker credentials or payment information.

Axion AI is a journaling assistant. Trade data you log is sent to an AI model only to produce your analysis. It is not stored by the AI provider or used to train models.

Axion AI cannot access live market data, connect to your broker, predict markets, or give investment advice.

Privacy Policy

Last updated · August 2025 · Axion Desk by Vibe Cartel Inc.

1. Who we are

Axion Desk is a personal AI-assisted trading journal operated by Vibe Cartel Inc. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. What we collect

  • Account data — name, email address (from signup / Google auth)
  • Journal data — trade logs, notes, strategies, psychology tags, and any other entries you create
  • Usage data — Axion Points, streak, preferences, theme, and settings you choose
  • Communication preferences — whether you opted into news, community digest, or tips emails
  • Technical data — basic device/browser info needed to run the service securely

We do not collect broker credentials, bank details, payment card numbers, or live market account information.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and improve the Axion Desk journaling and AI analysis features
  • To calculate points, streaks, and personal statistics
  • To send emails only if you explicitly opted in
  • To respond to support requests and keep the service secure

When you request an AI review, the relevant trade data you have logged is sent to an AI provider solely to generate that response. It is not retained by the provider for training.

4. Who can see your data

Your private Desk (trades, notes, AI sessions) is visible only to you. Axion Desk staff cannot browse your journal. Content you deliberately post on The Floor is visible to other members of the community.

// Your private Desk is yours. We are just holding it for you.

5. Data storage & security

Data is stored in a secure cloud database under your unique user ID. We use industry-standard security practices. No system is perfect; we continuously work to protect your information.

6. Your rights

  • Export — download your data as JSON or CSV from Settings at any time
  • Delete — permanently delete your account and associated data from Settings
  • Update — change your profile and preferences whenever you like
  • Opt out — change email preferences or contact us to stop communications

7. Cookies & local storage

We use local storage and similar technologies to remember your theme, preferences, and session so the app works smoothly. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will notify you in-app or by email (if you have opted in). Continued use after notice constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at co.vibecartel@gmail.com.
