Your private Desk — how your data is stored

What we store, how we store it, and what we never do with it.

▼

Your private trade logs, journal entries, and AI analysis sessions are stored in a secure database under your unique user ID. They are not visible to any other user, including Axion Desk staff, unless you explicitly share something on The Floor.

We store:

  • Your name and email address (from signup)
  • Trade logs and journal entries you create in your Desk
  • Axion Points and streak data
  • Your communication preferences (set on this page)
  • The date and time you accepted these terms

We do not sell, share, or monetise your personal data. We do not store broker credentials, financial account numbers, or payment information of any kind.

Your data is designed to be long-term safe — structured so it remains accessible and exportable for years. You can request a full export or deletion of your data at any time from your account settings.

Axion AI — what it is and what it isn't

How the AI analysis feature works under the hood.

▼

Axion AI is a journaling assistant. When you request an analysis of your trading session, the trade data you have logged for that session is sent to an AI model to generate a response. This data is used only to produce your analysis and is not stored by the AI provider or used to train any models.

Axion AI cannot:

  • Access live market data or real-time prices
  • Connect to your broker or trading account
  • Predict future market movements
  • Give personalised investment advice

Think of it as a mirror for your own trading behaviour — it reflects patterns in what you log, asks questions about your decisions, and helps you think more clearly. Nothing more.
